Snowflake sequence DDL is now rendered instead of refused: `CREATE SEQUENCE` carries `START WITH`, `INCREMENT BY`, `ORDER` / `NOORDER` and `IF NOT EXISTS`, `ALTER SEQUENCE` carries `INCREMENT BY` and `ORDER` / `NOORDER`, and `DROP SEQUENCE` carries `IF EXISTS`; the identity-column `ORDER` / `NOORDER` tail is now rendered after the parameter group (`IDENTITY(1, 1) ORDER` / `IDENTITY(1, 1) NOORDER`, reachable through the expression-layer `IdentityClause`).
