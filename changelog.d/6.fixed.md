Honour a declared `scale` that arrives without a `precision`: `SnowflakeNumberType(scale=2)` and `SnowflakeNumberType(scale=7)` used to render a bare `NUMBER` — one column, `NUMBER(38, 0)`, for two declarations `==` called different — and now render `NUMBER(38, 2)` and `NUMBER(38, 7)`, with core's declared default filling the missing precision (a scale with no precision and no dialect default still refuses, naming the field). The maximum scale follows the manual's 37 rather than 38, so `NUMBER(10, 38)` — a value the manual does not document — is refused naming the value and the limit instead of being written out.
